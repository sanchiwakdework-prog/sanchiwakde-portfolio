About

My career began in engineering: building data pipelines, cloud workflows and enterprise systems for large financial-services environments. At Deloitte, I learned that the quality of a technical solution is inseparable from the operating process, people and decisions around it.

I am most interested in the questions that sit before implementation: Where is the friction? What is the root cause? Which decision will create the most value—and what has to change for it to stick?

That perspective is shaping my next chapter: combining transformation delivery experience with structured business problem-solving, strategy and general management through my MMS at IIM Indore.

I bring a practical point of view to consulting work: enough technical depth to understand what is possible, and a growing business lens to focus change on the outcomes that matter.
